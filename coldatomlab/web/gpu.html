Preparation, numerical limits & performance

Periodic FFT boundaries are monitored. A boundary warning stops the run at the first step exceeding 0.1% probability in the outer band. A conserved norm alone does not establish spatial or time accuracy. Refine grid, domain, time step and preparation step for quantitative use.
