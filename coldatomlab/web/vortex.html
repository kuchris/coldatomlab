What this experiment models

The default stationary-vortex experiment compares core expansion with Lundh, Pethick & Smith (1998), Vortices in Bose-Einstein-Condensed Atomic Clouds. Its equations 36–39 and figure 5 use a radial wavefunction with a Gaussian axial profile. Our full 3D state does not impose that factorization. The dashed reference is computed from those reduced equations, not measured data. Finite grid resolution particularly affects the small initial core; the comparison is not a precision claim for arbitrary settings.

Full 3D conservative Gross–Pitaevskii evolution, i∂ψ/∂t = [−∇²/2 + V + g|ψ|²]ψ, with ∫|ψ|²d³r = 1 and g = 4πNaₛ/a₀. The selected Rb-87 trap has equal x/y frequencies; ω₀ is its radial angular frequency. Length a₀ = √(ℏ/mω₀), time t₀ = 1/ω₀ and energy ℏω₀. The harmonic trap stays on until Release trap switches off both trap and beam. Interactions remain on during TOF. No thermal cloud, loss, damping, quantum depletion or noise in the field evolution is included. Detector noise is applied only to captured images.

The rotating repulsive Gaussian is uniform along z: V = (x²+y²+λ²z²)/2 + H(t) exp[−((x−R cos Ωt)²+(y−R sin Ωt)²)/(2σ²)]. Its sin² ramps turn it on and off smoothly; λ is axial/radial trap frequency. No vortices are inserted during real-time evolution. Stationary preparation solves the centered charge sector and reports the full GPE residual. The optional legacy interacting imprint remains a prescribed initial state.

Phase winding is measured from the complex field around the declared contour. Flow circulation uses spectral derivatives; one circulation quantum is h/m. Lz is integrated over the full 3D field. A central-slice crossing does not reconstruct the complete 3D vortex line. Detection depends on density masking, interpolation and grid resolution. Dark holes alone do not establish a vortex.

The split-step Fourier solver uses periodic numerical boundaries and float32 arithmetic. It stops when |norm−1| or boundary probability exceeds 0.001; it never renormalizes real-time evolution. Physical time does not depend on animation speed. Reduce the time step and increase the grid/box to check convergence. Duration is rounded to the nearest full time step.

Madison, Chevy, Wohlleben & Dalibard (2000), Vortex formation in a stirred Bose–Einstein condensate motivates optical stirring. This orbiting-Gaussian teaching protocol is not a reconstruction of their apparatus, measured threshold or vortex lattice.
