What this experiment models

Full 3D conservative Gross–Pitaevskii evolution, i∂ψ/∂t = [−∇²/2 + V + g|ψ|²]ψ, with ∫|ψ|²d³r = 1 and g = 4πNaₛ/a₀. The Rb-87 reference trap is (50, 50, 100) Hz. Length a₀ = √(ℏ/mω₀), time t₀ = 1/ω₀ and energy ℏω₀. The harmonic trap stays on. No thermal cloud, loss, damping, quantum depletion or measurement noise is included.

The rotating repulsive Gaussian is uniform along z: V = (x²+y²+4z²)/2 + H(t) exp[−((x−R cos Ωt)²+(y−R sin Ωt)²)/(2σ²)]. Its sin² ramps turn it on and off smoothly. No vortices are inserted during real-time evolution. The interacting imprint is a prescribed initial state, not a stationary solution.

Phase winding is measured from the complex field around the declared contour. Flow circulation uses spectral derivatives; one circulation quantum is h/m. Lz is integrated over the full 3D field. A central-slice crossing does not reconstruct the complete 3D vortex line. Detection depends on density masking, interpolation and grid resolution. Dark holes alone do not establish a vortex.

The split-step Fourier solver uses periodic numerical boundaries and float32 arithmetic. It stops when |norm−1| or boundary probability exceeds 0.001; it never renormalizes real-time evolution. Physical time does not depend on animation speed. Reduce the time step and increase the grid/box to check convergence. Duration is rounded to the nearest full time step.

Madison, Chevy, Wohlleben & Dalibard (2000), Vortex formation in a stirred Bose–Einstein condensate motivates optical stirring. This orbiting-Gaussian teaching protocol is not a reconstruction of their apparatus, measured threshold or vortex lattice.
